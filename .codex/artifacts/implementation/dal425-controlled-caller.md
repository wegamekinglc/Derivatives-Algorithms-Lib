DAL-451 / DAL-425: S0L fixed caller allocation binding

Status: partial, unqualified. This private adapter adds no worker, product, compiler or arbitrary-command entry. It consumes the coordinator's actual S0L grant hash and ledger revision, rather than a local capacity fixture. The fixed trusted caller supplies the independently captured exact-task failure guard and its already durable allocation anchor. Changing the root, task, attempt, grant or ledger fails before dispatch. A permanent exclusive caller.pending records the full source/observer/profile/argv/environment/cwd binding and finite output plan. It is never removed or reused, including after success or interrupted fsync. The caller must preserve the same protected anchor across tasks; there is no recovery or capacity creation interface.

The caller must provide genuine measured identities and an output plan covering its actual writes; the adapter does not infer those facts from labels. Inputs, source generation and engineering artifacts preceding attachment remain the outer caller's responsibility. The 60 wall/core allocation stays fully held; the remaining 30 belongs only to independent review. Synthetic authority patched inside one test is not the actual grant or product budget.

The receipt collector saves the complete already acquired wait4 tuple before invoking pipe drain, JSON or logging. EAGAIN returns None, meaning pending, and is distinct from EOF. Other drain/logging failures propagate with the raw receipt retained. A receipt fsync failure prevents drain and poisons the attempt; spent slots are never refunded. The trusted foreground caller owns launch, exact PID/starttime checks, final wait4 and bounded collection. Inclusive observer usage includes its waited descendants and must not be added to their usage again.

Controls are confined to this adapter: missing shared allocation/guard, duplicate or changed-root consumption, EAGAIN, log-write failure, receipt fsync failure, deletion without refund and poisoned-attempt refusal. No old profile, inner, worker, G2 or numerical matrix is rerun. Three focused assertion failures precede the corresponding changes; the final direct suite has eight tests. Actual source and runtime identities plus publication facts belong in the issue evidence, not this reusable note.

Assumptions remain a private directory, one trusted writer, fixed host and CPython installation, immutable inputs during a call, and normal finite local I/O and scheduling. This adapter does not qualify the engineering observer's startup, ancestor, final tail, hidden Git execs or pre-attachment cumulative writes. An independent review must assess the actual outer receipts. Facility, H_exact/H_exclude and the four financial flags remain false. All historical UNKNOWNs and retained allocations remain unchanged.
