Calibrate_SingleCurve (in Excel: CALIBRATE.SINGLECURVE)

Calibrate a single yield curve from instruments and settings. Returns a result handle bundling the calibrated curve and the fit diagnostics.

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
todaydateThe valuation/trade date
ccystringCurrency code (e.g. "USD")
instrumentsvector of handlesArray of instrument handles (created with DEPOSIT.NEW, SWAP.NEW, etc.)
knotDatesvector of datesKnot dates (can be empty for auto-detection)
settingsmatrix of cellsyesOptional (key,value) settings. Keys: curveName, calibrateDiscountCurve, solveMode, parameterization, logDfScheme, smoothingWeight, tolerance, fitTolerance, initialGuess, maxEvaluations, maxRestarts, targetCollateral, targetTenor, liborBasis
discountCurvehandle of type StorableDiscountCurveyesOptional discount curve (OIS-collateralized) needed when calibrating a forward curve (calibrateDiscountCurve=FALSE)

Outputs:

NameType
resulthandle of type StorableCurveCalibrationResultThe calibration result (curve + diagnostics)
