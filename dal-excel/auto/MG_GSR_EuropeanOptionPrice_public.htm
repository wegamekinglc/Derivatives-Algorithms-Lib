GSR_EuropeanOptionPrice (in Excel: GSR.EUROPEANOPTIONPRICE)

Price a European rate option in a Gaussian short rate model

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
modelhandle of type ModelDataGSRModelData or MultiFactorGSRModelData
optionhandle of type StorableGSREuropeanOptionBond option, caplet or swaption
settingsmatrix of cellsyesKey/value rows: quadratureOrder (16), estimateError (true)

Outputs:

NameType
resultmatrix of cellsOne row: price, numerical refinement estimate
