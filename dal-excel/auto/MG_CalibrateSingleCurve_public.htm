CalibrateSingleCurve (in Excel: DA.CALIBRATESINGLECURVE)

Calibrate a single yield curve from instruments and settings. Returns the calibrated curve handle plus diagnostics (market rates, model rates, residuals, maxAbsResidual, rmsResidual)

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
todaydateThe valuation/trade date
ccystringCurrency code (e.g. "USD")
instrumentsvector of handlesArray of instrument handles (created with DA.DEPOSIT.NEW, DA.SWAP.NEW, etc.)
knotDatesvector of datesKnot dates (can be empty for auto-detection)
settingsmatrix of cellsyesOptional settings as a two-column range. Supported keys: curveName (string), calibrateDiscountCurve (boolean), smoothingWeight (number), tolerance (number), fitTolerance (number), maxEvaluations (integer), maxRestarts (integer), initialGuess (number), solveMode (string: EXACT|APPROXIMATE), parameterization (string: PIECEWISE_LINEAR_FWD|PIECEWISE_CONSTANT_FWD|ZERO_RATE|LOG_DISCOUNT), logDfScheme (string: LOG_LINEAR|LOG_CUBIC_NATURAL|MIXED), liborBasis (string), targetCollateral (string), targetTenor (string)
formatstringyes(Excel only) Desired screen layout of outputs; see QR.FORMAT.

Outputs:

NameType
curvehandle of type StorableDiscountCurveThe calibrated discount curve
marketRatesvector of numbersMarket quoted rates for each instrument
modelRatesvector of numbersModel-implied rates for each instrument
residualsvector of numbersRate residuals (model - market)
maxAbsResidualnumberMaximum absolute residual
rmsResidualnumberRoot-mean-square residual
