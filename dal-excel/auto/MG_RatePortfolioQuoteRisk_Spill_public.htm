RatePortfolioQuoteRisk_Spill (in Excel: RATEPORTFOLIOQUOTERISK.SPILL)

Aggregate portfolio quote sensitivity and DV01 into a fixed long-form spill

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
tradesvector of handlesRate trade definition handles
markethandle of type StorableRatePricingMarketThe rate pricing market used by the immutable provenance handles
provenancesvector of handlesQuote-risk provenance handles with unique non-empty calibration identifiers

Outputs:

NameType
spillmatrix of cellsTen columns in order: calibration, axis_fingerprint, quote_key, quote_name, block, currency, quote_sensitivity, dv01, availability, reason. Rows follow native deterministic bucket order, then provenance failures, trade/provenance failures, and excluded-domain failures.
