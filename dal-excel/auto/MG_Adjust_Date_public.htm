Adjust_Date (in Excel: ADJUST.DATE)

Adjust a date according to a business day convention

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
centerstringHoliday center name (e.g. "BEY" for Beijing, empty string for no holidays)
datedateThe date to adjust
conventionstringBusiness day convention: "Unadjusted", "Following", or "ModifiedFollowing"

Outputs:

NameType
resultdateThe adjusted date
