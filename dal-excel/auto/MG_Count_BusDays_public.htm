Count_BusDays (in Excel: COUNT.BUSDAYS)

Count business days between two dates

Inputs:

NameTypeOptional?
centerstringHoliday center name (e.g. "BEY" for Beijing, empty string for no holidays)
begindateThe start date (inclusive)
enddateThe end date (inclusive)

Outputs:

NameType
resultintegerThe number of business days in the range [begin, end]
